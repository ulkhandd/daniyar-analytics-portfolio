# Financial Forecasting & Valuation Model Outline

## Workbook Structure

1. Historical financials
2. Forecast assumptions
3. Revenue projection
4. Operating-profit projection
5. Free cash flow bridge
6. WACC calculation
7. DCF valuation
8. NPV and IRR sensitivity
9. Investment interpretation

## Core Formulas

- Cost of equity: `risk_free_rate + beta * equity_risk_premium`
- WACC: `E/(D+E) * cost_of_equity + D/(D+E) * cost_of_debt * (1 - tax_rate)`
- Enterprise value: sum of discounted forecast free cash flows plus discounted terminal value
- NPV: discounted cash inflows minus initial investment
- IRR: discount rate that sets NPV to zero
