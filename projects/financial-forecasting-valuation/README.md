# Financial Forecasting & Valuation Model

## Business Question

What is the implied value of a public company under a multi-year forecast, discounted cash-flow assumptions, and cost-of-capital estimates?

## Methods

- Built a multi-year forecast through 2025
- Projected revenue, operating profit, and comprehensive income
- Estimated WACC using CAPM cost of equity and cost of debt assumptions
- Calculated NPV and IRR for investment analysis
- Used DCF logic to connect operating forecasts to valuation conclusions
